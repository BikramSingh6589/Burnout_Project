# Burnout Score Engine

Formula

Burnout Score =
30% Stress
+
20% Sleep
+
15% Motivation
+
15% Attendance
+
10% Backlog
+
10% Sentiment

Output:

0–30 → Low Risk

31–60 → Moderate Risk

61–100 → High Risk
