# Lumina Focus Design System

## Brand & Style

The design system is built to evoke a sense of proactive recovery and cognitive clarity.

**Brand Personality**

* Innovative
* Reliable
* Supportive

The visual style combines:

* Minimalism
* Glassmorphism

Goals:

* Reduce cognitive load through whitespace
* Use vibrant translucent accents to guide attention
* Avoid dull healthcare-app aesthetics
* Create a tech-forward wellness experience
* Help users feel empowered, focused, and organized


### Level 2 (Modals / Popovers)

* Stronger shadow
* 1px border: `#F1F5F9`

### Glassmorphism Layer


* Navigation bars
* Floating action panels
* Focus containers

## Design Principles Summary

1. Minimal and distraction-free
2. Tech-forward wellness aesthetic
3. Large whitespace for reduced cognitive load
4. Electric Indigo as the action-driving color
5. Glassmorphism for modern depth
6. Rounded interfaces for friendliness
7. Strong readability through Hanken Grotesk, Inter, and Geist
8. Consistent 8px spacing system
9. High contrast for accessibility
10. Focus on helping users move from overwhelm to clarity
