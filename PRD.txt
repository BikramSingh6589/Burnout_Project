# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Academic Burnout Prediction and Monitoring Platform

Version: 1.0

Owner: Student Wellness Project Team

---

# 1. Product Overview

## Product Name

Academic Burnout Prediction Platform

## Product Vision

Help students identify and prevent academic burnout before it significantly impacts academic performance, mental well-being, and motivation.

The platform continuously monitors academic, behavioral, and emotional indicators to estimate burnout risk and provide actionable recommendations.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Many students experience burnout due to:

* Excessive coursework
* Continuous examinations
* Poor sleep habits
* Academic pressure
* Lack of motivation
* Anxiety and stress

Most students realize they are burned out only after:

* Grades drop
* Attendance decreases
* Motivation disappears
* Stress becomes overwhelming

Current educational systems rarely provide early warning mechanisms.

The platform aims to detect these warning signs early.
