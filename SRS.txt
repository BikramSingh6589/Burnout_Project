# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

# Academic Burnout Prediction and Monitoring Platform

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Purpose

The purpose of this system is to monitor student well-being, identify early signs of academic burnout, and provide personalized recommendations to reduce burnout risk before academic performance deteriorates.

The platform continuously analyzes student behavior, self-reported assessments, academic performance, and emotional indicators to generate a Burnout Risk Score.

---

## 1.2 Scope

The Academic Burnout Prediction Platform is a web-based application that enables students and educational institutions to:

* Assess burnout levels using scientifically inspired indicators.
* Track academic and lifestyle behaviors.
* Visualize burnout trends over time.
* Predict burnout risk levels.
* Provide personalized recommendations.
* Alert students when burnout risk becomes high.

The system does not provide medical diagnosis. It only estimates burnout risk based on collected indicators.

---

## 1.3 Objectives

The system shall:

* Detect early burnout symptoms.
* Monitor stress and wellness trends.
* Improve student productivity and mental well-being.
* Reduce academic performance decline.
* Encourage healthier study habits.

Example:

1–10

The system shall collect:

* Attendance percentage

### Grades

## FR-4 Lifestyle Tracking

The system shall record:

* Sleep hours
* Sleep quality
* Daily study hours
* Exercise duration
* Screen time

---

## FR-5 Mood Journal

Students shall be able to submit daily journal entries.

Example:

"I feel exhausted and overwhelmed by assignments."

Stored Data:

* Date
* Text Entry

---

## FR-6 Sentiment Analysis Module

The system shall analyze mood journal text.

Detected Features:

* Positive sentiment
* Neutral sentiment
* Negative sentiment

Stress Keywords:

* tired
* exhausted
* overwhelmed
* burnout
* hopeless
* anxious

Output:

Sentiment Score

Range:

0–100

---

## FR-7 Burnout Score Calculation

The system shall calculate a burnout score.

Formula:

Burnout Score =
(30% Stress Score)
+
(20% Sleep Score)
+
(15% Motivation Score)
+
(15% Attendance Score)
+
(10% Assignment Backlog Score)
+
(10% Sentiment Score)

Output Range:

0–100

---

## FR-8 Risk Classification

### Low Risk

0–30

### Moderate Risk

31–60

### High Risk

61–100

---

## FR-9 Trend Analysis

The system shall track burnout progression.

Weekly Burnout History:

Week 1 → Score 25

Week 2 → Score 38

Week 3 → Score 52

Week 4 → Score 69

The system shall identify:

* Increasing trend
* Stable trend
* Decreasing trend

---

## FR-10 Recommendation Engine

The platform shall provide recommendations.

Examples:

If Sleep < 5 Hours:

Recommendation:

"Improve sleep duration to at least 7 hours."

If Stress > 8:

Recommendation:

"Consider stress management exercises."

If Attendance < 60%:

Recommendation:

"Focus on attending upcoming classes regularly."
