#System Requirement Specification (SRS)

# Academic Burnout Prediction and Monitoring Platform

Version: 1.0

Owner: Student Wellness Project Team

---

# 1. Product Overview

## Product Name

Academic Burnout Prediction and Monitoring Platform

## Product Vision

To help students identify, monitor, and prevent academic burnout through continuous behavioral analysis, emotional monitoring, personalized recommendations, and AI-assisted wellness support before burnout significantly impacts academic performance and well-being.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Students often experience burnout due to:

* Academic pressure
* Continuous examinations
* Poor sleep habits
* Excessive screen time
* Assignment overload
* Lack of motivation
* Stress and anxiety
* Poor study-life balance
* Procrastination

Most students recognize burnout only after:

* Grades decline
* Productivity decreases
* Attendance drops
* Motivation disappears
* Stress becomes overwhelming

Current systems provide little to no early-warning support.

The platform aims to detect burnout early and recommend effective interventions.

# 8. Burnout Intelligence Architecture

The platform uses a five-layer intelligence architecture.

---

## Layer 1: Rule-Based Burnout Scoring

Initial score based on weighted indicators.

Inputs:

* Stress
* Motivation
* Energy
* Academic Satisfaction
* Sleep
* Study Hours
* Screen Time
* Assignment Backlog
* Procrastination
* Mood Sentiment

---

## Layer 2: Personal Baseline Analysis

Creates a unique behavioral baseline for each student.

Purpose:

Detect changes relative to the student's own history.

Example:

Day 1

Sleep = 7

Stress = 4

Day 30

Sleep = 4

Stress = 8

The system identifies deterioration even if population averages remain normal.

---

## Layer 3: Population Machine Learning Model

Uses historical data from all users.

Factors:

* Age
* Sleep
* Stress
* Motivation
* Study Hours
* Screen Time
* Procrastination
* Mood Sentiment

Purpose:

Identify common burnout patterns.

---

## Layer 4: Feature Extraction Engine

Processes:

* AI conversations
* Mood journals
* Feedback submissions

Extracts:

* Stress indicators
* Sleep indicators
* Mood indicators
* Behavioral indicators

Only useful burnout-related information is retained.

---

## Layer 5: Recommendation Learning Engine

Tracks:

* Recommendations given
* User feedback
* Burnout score changes
* Mood improvements

Purpose:

Learn which interventions work best for each student.

---

# 9. Privacy Model

Student privacy is a core requirement.

Administrators can view:

* Burnout score
* Risk level
* Sentiment summaries
* Trend analytics

Administrators cannot view:

* Private AI conversations
* Raw mood journal content

The system stores only relevant extracted wellness indicators for prediction purposes.
